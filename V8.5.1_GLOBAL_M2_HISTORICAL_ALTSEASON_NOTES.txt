V8.5.1 GLOBAL M2 + HISTORICAL ALTSEASON CALIBRATION

1. Global M2
- Global M2 is NOT an official single IMF/FRED series.
- This version builds a transparent USD monthly index from primary public sources:
  US M2 + Euro Area M2 + Japan M2 + UK M4.
- China is excluded because the public automated PBoC endpoint is not stable enough for a dependable browser/Worker pipeline.
- Formula:
  Global M2 = US M2 + (Euro Area M2 * USD/EUR) + (Japan M2 / JPY per USD) + (UK M4 * USD/GBP)
- US M2: FRED M2SL
- Euro Area M2: ECB Data Portal BSI M2 stocks
- Japan M2: Bank of Japan Time-Series Data Search API, M2 average outstanding
- UK M4: Bank of England LPMAUYN, seasonally adjusted monthly M4
- FX: FRED USD/EUR, JPY/USD, USD/GBP series.

2. Current ALTSEASON SCORE
- Components are normalized to 100 from the original 80 raw points:
  Liquidity 20
  Rates/Dollar 20
  BTC trend 10
  BTC Dominance 20
  ETH/BTC rotation 10
- Current ETH/BTC is calculated from Upbit KRW-ETH / KRW-BTC prices.
- Current BTC.D remains CoinGecko-based.

3. Historical ALTSEASON SCORE
- Annual calibration table is generated for 2011-2026.
- Global M2 annual growth is calculated from the reconstructed monthly Global M2 series.
- BTC.D annual averages 2013-2025 use CoinGecko's published historical annual data.
- ETH/BTC annual averages 2015-2025 use CoinGecko's published historical annual data.
- BTC annual return anchors are used for the historical BTC-trend component.
- 2011-2012 are shown as reference only because comparable BTC.D/ETH-BTC alt-market breadth did not yet exist.
- The historical table is a calibration/backtest view, not a predictive guarantee.

4. Important interpretation
- The historical score is intended to answer: "When the market actually experienced broad alt strength, how high would this rule-based score have risen?"
- It should be used to calibrate thresholds, not to claim that a particular score predicts future returns.
- The next research step would be a monthly/daily full backtest with actual historical BTC.D, ETH/BTC, BTC, alt-market volume and stablecoin liquidity series wherever licensing/public access permits.
